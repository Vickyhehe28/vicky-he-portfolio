overview: "Rolli Bakehouse is a home based bakery built from the ground up. The project focused on creating a warm, artisan-inspired brand identity that reflects its homemade roots and community spirit."
challenge: "As a zero-to-one business, there was no existing brand direction to build from. The identity also needed to translate seamlessly into in-person event promotions while maintaining consistency across all touchpoints."
solution: "Develop a memorable and inviting brand that communicates authenticity and craftsmanship. Build a cohesive visual system adaptable across digital, print, and in-person experiences."

marquee: ["Dacquoise", "Madeleine", "Scone", "Cake roll"]

system:
  intro: "The brand’s color palette is inspired by the warmth and authenticity of artisanal baking. Brown reflects natural ingredients and handcrafted quality, orange brings energy and playfulness, and blue adds a calm, refined contrast. Together, they balance nature with a modern aesthetic, creating a brand that feels playful, elevated, and thoughtfully crafted."
