overview: "Sky Builders is an immersive, child-centered exhibition inspired by the sculptural language of David Smith. The project translates his industrial steel sculptures into a colorful, interactive environment where children explore balance, verticality, and negative space through movement and play."
challenge: "David Smith’s work is often perceived as heavy, industrial, and serious. The challenge was translating those concepts into a playful, safe, and interactive experience while still preserving the core sculptural ideas of structure, balance, and spatial composition."
solution: "To reinterpret modernist sculpture in a way that is accessible and engaging for children by transforming traditional gallery viewing into an experiential learning environment."
